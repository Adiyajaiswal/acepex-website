10 — Questions

Answers before you ask.

Common questions about working with ACEPEX.

What does ACEPEX do?

ACEPEX builds software, automation and internal business systems that replace repetitive work, and runs the marketing that brings customers in: funnels, lead generation and social media. Everything connects into one system, from the first enquiry to the paid invoice.

Where is ACEPEX based?

ACEPEX is based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. You can start a conversation over WhatsApp, a call or email.

Which businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses that run on enquiries, bookings and payments, such as real estate firms, hostels and PGs, coworking spaces and service businesses.

Do you build marketing funnels and run lead generation?

Yes. We design the full funnel from first click to paying customer: the offer, landing page, lead capture, automatic WhatsApp and email follow-up, and booking. Lead generation campaigns on Meta, Google and WhatsApp feed the same system, so every enquiry reaches your CRM.

Can you handle our social media?

Yes. We handle content planning, posts, reels and replies, and send a regular report on reach and enquiries. Because it connects to your lead system, you can see which content brings real enquiries.

Can you build a CRM for real estate?

Yes. We build lead pipelines that capture enquiries from portals, ads, WhatsApp and calls, assign them to agents, schedule site visits, and track negotiations, bookings and documents.

Do you build hostel or PG management software?

Yes. A hostel system can handle live bed availability, bookings, check-in and check-out, rent reminders, maintenance tickets and an owner dashboard for occupancy and revenue.

How does a project start?

We begin by understanding how your business runs today, then design, build and deploy the system. You can start on WhatsApp or by email and describe the work you want to remove.
